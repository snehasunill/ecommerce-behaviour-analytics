Method notes

Stages in the ordered visitor funnel are based on timestamps; they do not imply a single session. First-observed visitor cohorts are not signup cohorts. Engagement groups use a pre-outcome observation window, but the conversion difference is associative, not causal. Item views and purchase counts are event-based, not unique product-buyer conversion probabilities. The dataset has no experiment assignments or verified revenue.
